# IOLab — Intelligent Optimization Laboratory

**University of Udine, Italy** · [iolab.uniud.it](https://iolab.uniud.it/)

IOLab studies and develops optimization methods for complex decision-making in industrial and organizational contexts, combining operations research with artificial intelligence.

## About us

IOLab evolved from the *Scheduling and Timetabling Research Group*, founded in 2000, and was rebranded as IOLab in 2023 through the University's Strategic Plan in Artificial Intelligence.

Members include Luca Di Gaspero, Sara Ceschia, Andrea Schaerf, Francesca Da Ros, and others.

## Research topics

- Metaheuristics and hybrid optimization methods
- Data-driven approaches
- Large language models in optimization
- Healthcare planning (home care, emergency systems)
- Sustainable logistics (electric-vehicle routing, waste collection)
- Industrial production (energy-aware scheduling)
- Educational and workforce timetabling
- Maritime operations and safety-critical systems

## Recent achievements

- Francesca Da Ros won the 2026 AIROYoung Dissertation Award.
- The railway capacity paper received the Best Application Paper Runner-Up award at CP 2026.
